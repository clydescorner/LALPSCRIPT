About

Welcome to the Text-to-XML Converter created for the research project The Language of the Labouring Poor in Late Modern England.

The SNSF-funded project The Language of the Labouring Poor in Late Modern English, running from March 2020 to February 2025, aims to determine the role of social stratification in language variation and change during the period c. 1795–1834.
